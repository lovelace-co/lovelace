# ADR-0004: Lexical with the byte-reuse reconciler

## Status

Accepted. Supersedes the editing-surface half of ADR-0003; the block model and round-trip bar from ADR-0002 stand.

## Context

The hand-rolled contenteditable editor (ADR-0003) protected the round-trip bar but failed dogfooding on editing quality: caret, selection, undo and IME are a product of their own. The user chose Lexical (MIT, Meta) over Tiptap, whose core is also MIT but whose pro tier raised licensing concern, and over Milkdown, whose serialiser takes control of the normal form.

## Decision

The editing surface is Lexical with the playground shape: a formatting toolbar plus inline Notion-style editing, official markdown import/export, markdown shortcut typing, check lists, history. Two layers of ours sit around it:

1. Verbatim protection: blocks outside the editable subset (tables, raw HTML) are swapped for sentinels before conversion and ride through the editor as a custom VerbatimNode, exporting their original bytes.
2. The byte-reuse reconciler: on save, each output block whose Lexical normal form matches an original block (in order, via a headless Lexical instance) re-emits the original's exact bytes. Unedited blocks never change; an unchanged document never saves.

## Consequences

- Editing quality is Lexical's, not ours; the converters we maintain shrink to one transformer and a reconciler.
- The normal form is Lexical's. The reconciler confines normalisation to edited blocks, which the minimal-diff bar permits.
- The editor dependency is swappable in principle (the reconciler is editor-agnostic), but selection of Lexical is a real commitment: theme, toolbar and the verbatim node are Lexical-specific.
- In-place table editing remains deferred; tables stay verbatim.
