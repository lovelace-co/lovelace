## Description

Implement the approved design modernisation. Direction: modernise by subtraction in regions and chrome, with legible species-specific forms on controls. Colour tokens, Geist, and every design law in CLAUDE.md stay; the execution changes.

The governing rules:

- Every control species has its own form: capsule to press, 9px well to type, well with caret to choose, 8px seat for rows, typographic tertiary actions.
- One surface per region: no wells holding cards holding chips.
- State moves, never marks: no left rails, no selection dots, no underline focus. Hover materialises or brightens, the nav seat glides, press punches. Focus stays the full cyan ring.
- Seven-step spacing scale (4/8/14/22/36/58/94) and one radius per species.
- Surfaces bleed into the gutter so text keeps the shared left edge.

Five phase tickets under this epic, strictly ordered. Reference: the modernisation proposal artefact (claude.ai/code/artifact/de14ecda-287c-4702-b274-ddac56036c42), including its section 07 law-by-law compliance sweep.
