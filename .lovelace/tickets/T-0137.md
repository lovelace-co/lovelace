## What happens

In the New diagram modal, the template chooser row looks out of place: the label reads lowercase "template", and on hover the row grows a tinted surface with no horizontal padding, so the label sits flush against its left edge.

## Cause

Two defects compound:

- `apps/desktop/src/components/MermaidEditModal.tsx` renders the literal lowercase string "template"; labels elsewhere are Title Case ("Project name", "Your name"), and the conventions forbid raw lowercase identifiers in the UI.
- The schema editor's field list added unscoped rules at `apps/desktop/src/styles/app.css:4212` (`.field-row { text-align: left; transition: ... }` and `.field-row:hover { background: var(--wash) }`). Those were meant for its own button rows but apply to every plain `field-row` in the app: New ticket, Init wizard, General settings, Documents frontmatter and the mermaid modal, all of which gain a spurious hover wash.

## Fix

- Title-case the label to "Template".
- Scope the schema editor rules to `button.field-row`, which matches exactly its interactive rows (`field-row field-row-grid` and `field-row field-row-header` buttons) and nothing else; the locked rows (`field-row-grid field-row-locked`, no `field-row` class) keep their current no-hover behaviour.

## Acceptance

- The template row shows "Template" and no hover wash appears on form rows in modals, settings or the wizard.
- The schema editor's field rows keep their hover wash and left-aligned text.
- `pnpm test` (desktop) and `pnpm build` pass.
