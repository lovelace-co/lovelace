## Context

The live-work ring is a conic gradient whose start angle animates linearly. A conic gradient advances by angle, not by distance: on anything but a square, equal angle steps sweep unequal lengths of border, so the comet races across the short edges and corners and crawls along the middles of the long edges. Cards are roughly 2.5:1 and list rows far wider, so the unevenness is plainly visible.

## Decision

The comet travels the border at constant path speed instead. Each live surface carries a small absolutely positioned SVG (a shared component) with a rounded rectangle stroke normalised by pathLength, and the dash offset animates linearly, which is uniform in arc length by construction for any aspect ratio and follows the corner radii exactly. Two stacked dashes fake the gradient tail: a longer soft dash under a short bright head sharing the same start, so one animation drives both. The conic rules, the orbit angle property and the old keyframes are removed.

## Acceptance criteria

- Board cards and list rows show the ring moving at visually constant speed around the whole border, same 7 second lap, same direction of travel as today, colours from the existing tokens only.
- The rounded corners of each species (card radius, seat radius) are followed exactly.
- Reduced motion shows the static full ring as today.
- The old conic ring rules and the registered orbit property are gone; no duplicated geometry between card and row.
- Rendering tests cover the ring appearing on live cards and rows and not otherwise.
- Desktop tests pass; the app bundles cleanly.

## Non-goals

No change to what counts as live, no change to the detail-header readout, no new colours or timing values.
