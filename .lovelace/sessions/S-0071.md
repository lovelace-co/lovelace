## Approach

Add multi-select to the List view with bulk delete and bulk move to another status group, delegated to a programmer subagent from a scouted file map and audited afterwards.

## What happened

Selection follows the state-moves-never-marks rule: cmd or ctrl click toggles a row, shift click extends the range across group boundaries in visible order, a plain click opens the ticket and clears the selection, Escape clears. A floating bulk bar (pop palette, radius-float) shows the count, a Move to dropdown of status labels, Delete and Clear. Bulk delete confirms through a modal that sums comments and sessions across the selection; bulk move calls updateTicket per id with the human actor, delete closes the open ticket if it was among the ids. Audit found one defect the subagent missed: Escape while the delete modal was open cleared the selection and left the modal over zero tickets; fixed so Escape closes the modal first, with a regression test. 336 tests green, build clean.
