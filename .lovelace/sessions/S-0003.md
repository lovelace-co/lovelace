## Approach

Bundle the Bun sidecars into the Tauri app via externalBin, add the updater plugin with a generated keypair, produce a real release build, and document signing, notarisation and website publishing.

## What happened

Release .app embeds all three sidecars and the embedded host answers requests. Updater artifact built and signed (Lovelace.app.tar.gz plus .sig); DMG produced with hdiutil after the Finder-based Tauri DMG step failed headless, workaround documented in docs/RELEASING.md. Code signing and notarisation need Apple and Windows certificates and are documented as manual steps.
