## What happened

Full test suite and build green. The initial tap content (cask for 0.1.0 with real checksums plus a readme) is committed in a local repo ready to push. Two steps remain and both need the user: creating the public lovelace-co/homebrew-tap repository (the permission classifier blocked gh repo create) and minting the fine-grained PAT for the HOMEBREW_TAP_TOKEN secret.
